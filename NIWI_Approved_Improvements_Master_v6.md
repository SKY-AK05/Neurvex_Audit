# NIWI --- Approved Assessment Improvements

## 1. Save & Resume Assessment

### Problem

Respondents may not be able to complete the NIWI assessment in one
sitting. If they leave halfway---because they are busy, close the
browser, lose connection, or need more time---they risk losing their
progress or having to start again. NIWI must also handle multiple
respondents filling out the assessment at the same time and reliably
save each person's progress without losing or mixing responses.

### Solution

Provide a Google Forms-style automatic save and resume experience:

-   Automatically save answers as respondents fill in the assessment,
    without requiring them to click Save.
-   Allow respondents to leave at any point and return later to continue
    where they stopped, with their answers restored.
-   Send email reminders to respondents who started but have not
    completed the assessment, prompting them to return and finish.
-   Automatically delete incomplete drafts after 30 days.
-   Handle multiple respondents filling in the assessment
    simultaneously, saving each person's progress reliably without lost,
    mixed, or overwritten responses and without excessive backend load.

## 2. Assessment Progress Indicator

### Problem

Respondents may not have a clear visual indication of how far they have
progressed through the NIWI assessment or how many questions they have
answered and still need to complete.

### Solution

Add a progress indicator to the assessment interface. A colored progress
line along the top should move forward as the respondent completes
questions, and a question count should show how many questions have been
answered and/or remain. Let the development agent decide the exact
placement of the count, colors, and visual styling so that it fits
naturally into the existing NIWI design and remains clear and easy to
use.

## 3. Privacy Policy Link

### Problem

Respondents are asked to consent to the collection and processing of
their organisational data in accordance with the Privacy Policy, but the
policy should be directly accessible from the consent statement so they
can review it before agreeing.

### Solution

Embed the Privacy Policy URL as a clickable hyperlink on the words
**"Privacy Policy"** in the consent statement shown in the assessment:

https://www.orchvate.com/Privacy_Policy.html

The link should open the policy for respondents to review, while keeping
the consent statement clear and easy to read.
